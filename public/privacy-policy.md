# Privacy Policy for Tic Attack Toe

**Last updated: May 5, 2026**

## Overview

Tic Attack Toe ("the App") is operated by Squire Studio. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you create a profile, we collect:
- A gamertag (display name) you choose
- A hashed PIN (we never store your PIN in plain text)
- An avatar image generated from a word you provide

**Game Data**
We collect and store:
- Game results (wins, losses, games played)
- Ranked Points (RP) and leaderboard standings

**Device Data**
We store the following locally on your device only:
- Your profile information
- A cache of AI-generated images to reduce load times

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

## How We Use Your Information

- To display your gamertag and avatar on the leaderboard
- To track your game stats and ranking
- To enable multiplayer game sessions

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

- **Anthropic** — powers AI battle narratives (anthropic.com/privacy)
- **Fal.ai** — generates AI artwork for game objects (fal.ai/privacy)
- **Upstash** — stores leaderboard and multiplayer game state (upstash.com/trust/privacy.pdf)
- **Google AdMob** — serves advertisements (policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data Retention

Your profile and game stats are stored on our servers indefinitely unless you request deletion. Multiplayer game rooms are automatically deleted after 30 minutes.

## Children's Privacy

The App is rated for users aged 9 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

## Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
