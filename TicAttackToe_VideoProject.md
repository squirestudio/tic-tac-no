# Tic Attack Toe — Video Ad Project

## Overview
A 60-second Pixar-style 3D animated short promoting the game, with shorter cuts for social media and ads. Three anthropomorphic characters meet in a park to play Tic Attack Toe on a chalk board drawn on a stone table. When a player makes a move, their word materializes as the actual object. One player wins tic-tac-toe while the other two are distracted battling each other.

---

## Characters

**GORILLA 🦍**
Large silverback in a worn brown cardigan, round reading glasses perched low on nose, enormous hands that make the chalk look comically tiny. Deeply serious. Moves with slow deliberate gravitas. The chess grandmaster energy.

**PENCIL ✏️**
Tiny anthropomorphic yellow No. 2 pencil, pink eraser for a head, small white gloves, carries a miniature leather briefcase. Permanently smug. Writes by pressing its own sharpened tip directly to the board — its whole body is the instrument.

**POOP 💩**
Large swirly brown emoji, big white expressive eyes, backwards baseball cap. Chaotic, emotional, reckless confidence. Plays big disaster words with zero strategy.

**Story arc:** Gorilla and Poop are so consumed battling each other with increasingly epic words (THUNDER vs VOLCANO, GLACIER vs METEOR) that the Pencil quietly wins the top row with three humble words. Classic underdog beat.
